01  /  The Idea

It started as a head I could not stop rebuilding.

Zmago began with one question: could a dragon built entirely from standard bricks still move like an animal? The jaw came first, then the eyes, and after that the rest of the body had to earn its place around them.

02  /  The Build

Built forward from the skull, one segment at a time.

03  /  The Details

Everything that moves, moves on purpose.

01
Poseable head and jaw

The skull turns on a click hinge and the lower jaw opens far enough to show the red tongue.

02
Articulated neck and tail

Segmented joints along the spine hold a pose without sagging, from a low stalk to a raised S-curve.

03
Folding wings

Layered blue and gold panels fan open on a shared axle and tuck flat against the body.

04
Four gripping claws

Each foot is built from gold clips and white horns, so the weight sits on the toes rather than a plate.
